What we’re building

A new network for a less weird society

We’re designing a global network where connection doesn’t require selling your attention. Talk freely. Stay private. Skip the manipulation layer.

No ads whispering in your ear. Hard pass.

“We cannot have a society in which the only way two people communicate is if it’s financed by a third person who wishes to manipulate them.”

— Jaron Lanier

Our mission

Privacy first. People first. Utopia later (maybe).

Our mission is to design and build a global network where people connect without worrying about their privacy — and then stack better tools for a better kind of connection on top.

Ambitious? Absolutely. Creepy surveillance capitalism? Not invited.

Why WAAC

Because we really are all connected

“We are all connected; to each other, biologically. To the earth, chemically. To the rest of the universe, atomically.”

— Neil deGrasse Tyson

That quote isn’t decoration — it’s the brief. Technology should honor the connection, not exploit it.
